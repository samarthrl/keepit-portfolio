02 — The problem

Useful things end up scattered.

A thought in one place, a photo in another, a document somewhere else, a location you meant to remember. When the things you keep live in different apps, they are harder to find again.

03 — The solution

One simple space to keep it all.

KEEPIT gives every kind of keep a clear home. One quick action starts any capture, categories keep things tidy, and search brings them back.

  • One “Keep something” action for photo, voice, note, PDF or place
  • Categories such as Personal, Work, Study, Home, Documents and Ideas
  • A single list of everything you choose to remember

05 — App experience

From first sign-in
to your keeps.

Every screen below is shown in full, uncropped. Move your cursor over a screen to tilt it.

01 · CREATE ACCOUNT

Start with your space.

Create an account with a name, email and password, or continue with your phone, to begin saving and organizing personal content.

02 · LOGIN

Welcome back.

Sign in with email and password, or continue with phone. A “Forgot password?” option is one tap away.

03 · HOME

A simple starting point.

One bold “Keep something” button, quick Photo, Voice and Note shortcuts, and a “Coming up” area for dates from your keeps.

04 · QUICK ACTIONS

Keep something.

“What do you want to keep?” Choose Photo, Voice, PDF, Note or Place, each with its own colour so you always know where you are.

05 · YOUR KEEPS

Everything together.

A running count of saved keeps, All keeps and Favorites filters, and a card for each keep with star, reminder and delete actions.

06 · EDIT NOTE

Capture the thought.

Give a note a title, write the details and file it under a category: Personal, Work, Study, Home, Documents or Ideas.

07 · SEARCH

Find what you kept.

Search by title, category or content and bring saved keeps straight back.

08 · VOICE

Save it in your voice.

“Capture your voice.” Tap the microphone and start speaking, add a title, then pick a category such as Personal, Work, Study, Home, Ideas or Important.

09 · PDF

Keep documents close.

Choose an existing PDF and KEEPIT copies it into its own secure app storage so it stays available after you restart the app.

10 · PLACE

Save where you are.

Keep a useful place now and find it again whenever you need it, with current location, place name, category and address.

11 · SETTINGS

Your app, your controls.

Profile, Notifications, Privacy, Backup, About and Logout, all in one clear list.

Categories, favorites and search.

Every keep carries a category, so Personal, Work, Study, Home, Documents and Ideas stay separate. Star the ones that matter, add a reminder, and search by title, category or content.
